Your Personal Net Worth Dashboard

Vaultic is a self-hosted personal finance dashboard that aggregates all your accounts in one place — bank accounts, investments, retirement, crypto, and mortgage — with net worth history, allocation, market data and news on one screen.

🏦 Plaid-connected accounts — Chase, Vanguard, Robinhood, and more
📊 Market summary and news for the tickers you hold
📈 Net worth history — daily snapshots, multi-year charts
📄 PDF import — parse investment statements automatically
🔐 JWT + bcrypt + TOTP 2FA — security-first from day one
☁️ Continuous backup — Litestream to Cloudflare R2
